🛡️ 1. Privacy by Design (Summary)

Xakame was engineered with a strict local-first architecture. We believe your digital habits, screen time, and personal app usage belong entirely to you.

100% On-Device Processing: Xakame does NOT collect, sell, lease, or transmit your personal data, app usage statistics, browsing history, or contact information to any external server or third party.

  • No User Accounts Required: You don't need to sign in, provide an email, or link a phone number.
  • Zero Tracking & Zero Ads: We do not embed ad SDKs, analytics trackers, or profiling cookies.
  • Complete Local Storage: All time limits, logs, and focus sessions are stored securely in your device's local database.

⚙️ 2. Android Permissions & Justification

In order to provide real-time app limits and distraction blocking, Xakame requests specific Android system permissions. Below is an explicit breakdown of how each permission is used:

Usage Access PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS

Purpose: Calculates the cumulative daily time you spend on restricted apps (e.g., social media or games).

Safety: Only reads foreground duration timestamps from Android's system API. It never accesses what you view or type inside other apps.

Accessibility Service BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE

Purpose: Detects when a locked app is opened by the user to immediately display the Xakame blocking screen, and prevents circumvention/self-sabotage during active Focus Mode sessions.

Safety: Xakame strictly inspects the package name of the active foreground window. It never reads keystrokes, personal messages, banking details, passwords, or on-screen content.

Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Purpose: Sends you alerts when you are nearing your daily limit, when Focus Mode starts/ends, and when daily limits reset at midnight.

🔒 3. Data Storage & Deletion

All app state, limit rules, and statistics are stored locally on your device using Android's sandbox storage.

  • Total Data Control: You can reset all app statistics anytime from the in-app settings.
  • Instant Erase: Uninstalling Xakame immediately and permanently deletes all application data from your device.

👶 4. Children's Privacy

Xakame does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Since no personal information is gathered from any user, Xakame is safe for general and family use.
